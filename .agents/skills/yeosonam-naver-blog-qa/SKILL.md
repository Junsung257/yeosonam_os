# Yeosonam Naver Blog QA

Use this only as a review contract. External Naver Blog publication is outside the current autopilot release.

## Checks

- The title reads naturally and does not repeat target keywords mechanically.
- The opening and subheading pattern differ materially from recent posts on the same account.
- Destination, route, season, audience, and decision details are specific and source-bounded.
- Tables and checklists exist only when they help the reader; generic template blocks are removed.
- Advertising claims and CTAs are proportionate, varied, and clearly separated from factual guidance.
- The draft is not a sentence-level rewrite of the first-party blog, a cafe post, or another account post.

Record failures and suggested claim-preserving edits. Never add engagement automation, Selenium posting, comments, likes, neighbor requests, credentials, or external publication side effects.
